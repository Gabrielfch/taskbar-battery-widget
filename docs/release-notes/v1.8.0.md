## Taskbar Battery Widget 1.8.0

### New
- **Battery statistics.** The *…* button of a device with the detailed log turned on now opens a statistics
  window built from its log: number of charges, equivalent charge cycles, estimated runtime on a full charge,
  average time between charges, average drain (%/h), average charge time and gain, and last charge. Charts show
  the battery level (with charging periods shaded), the daily drain and the daily charges for the last 24 h,
  7 days, 30 days or all the data. *Open log folder* is in the same window.
- **Time left.** Devices with the detailed log turned on show an estimate in the device panel and on the hover
  card: "≈ 6 h left" while discharging, "full in ≈ 40 min" while charging. It is computed in the background from
  the last 7 days of the log and appears once there are at least 6 h of discharge and a 10 % drop.
- **Full-charge notification.** *Settings ▸ Alerts ▸ Notify when fully charged* (on by default) notifies once
  per charge when a device reaches 100 %, or stops charging at 99 % or more.

### Changed
- The device panel stays open while the *Settings* menu, *About* or a statistics window is open; it closes when
  you click outside the app or press Esc.
- The app's windows have their own icon and taskbar entry.

### Fixed
- Razer Basilisk V3 Pro over the 2.4 GHz dongle confirmed working
  ([#1](https://github.com/Gabrielfch/taskbar-battery-widget/issues/1)).

### Update
- From 1.4.0 or later: *About… ▸ Check for updates ▸ Download and install* (signed update), or install the
  Setup below. Settings are kept.
- The executables are not Authenticode-signed: on PCs with Smart App Control turned on, Windows blocks them,
  including in-app updates.

### Verify your download
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of both executables; `SHA256SUMS.txt.sig` is its Ed25519 signature (see
  SECURITY.md for the public keys and manual verification).
- `gh attestation verify <file>.exe --repo Gabrielfch/taskbar-battery-widget` checks that the file was built by
  this repository's workflow.

### Downloads
- **TaskbarBatteryWidget-Setup-1.8.0.exe** — installer (upgrades previous versions and keeps your settings).
- **TaskbarBatteryWidget-portable-1.8.0.exe** — single executable, no installation.

If Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
