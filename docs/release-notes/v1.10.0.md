## Taskbar Battery Widget 1.10.0

### New
- **Position in fullscreen.** *Settings ▸ Personalization ▸ Position ▸ In fullscreen*: keep the usual place, or
  move the widget to one of 8 positions or a draggable floating spot while a game or video is fullscreen on its
  monitor (needs *Visible in fullscreen*). It returns to its usual place afterwards; positions dragged in
  fullscreen are stored separately.
- **Animated icons.** Custom icons can be animated GIF or WebP (up to 2 MB, 48 frames, 15 fps, 10 s); they are
  converted once on import.
- **Subtle animations.** A soft pulse on the charging bolt and on a critically low battery. *Personalization ▸
  Animations*: Always, Follow Windows (default) or Never. They pause over fullscreen apps and when nothing
  animated is visible (about 0.2 % of one CPU core with three animations).
- **Forget a device.** A trash button on *Known devices* removes a device with its alias, custom icon and
  settings (and, optionally, its detailed log) after a confirmation.

### Fixed
- A Logitech device (e.g. G915 keyboard, G903 mouse) charging over its USB cable no longer appears twice: it
  keeps its receiver identity, alias and icon, with the battery and charging state of the cable connection. The
  app remembers which receiver device each unit belongs to as a salted hash; the unit id itself is never stored
  or logged.
- A custom image whose extension does not match its content is stored with the right format instead of
  failing silently.

### Update
- From 1.4.0 or later: *About… ▸ Check for updates ▸ Download and install* (signed update), or install the
  Setup below. Settings are kept.
- The executables are not Authenticode-signed: on PCs with Smart App Control turned on, Windows blocks them,
  including in-app updates.

### Verify your download
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of both executables; `SHA256SUMS.txt.sig` is its Ed25519 signature (see
  SECURITY.md for the public keys and manual verification).
- `gh attestation verify <file>.exe --repo Gabrielfch/taskbar-battery-widget` checks that the file was built by
  this repository's workflow.

### Downloads
- **TaskbarBatteryWidget-Setup-1.10.0.exe** — installer (upgrades previous versions and keeps your settings).
- **TaskbarBatteryWidget-portable-1.10.0.exe** — single executable, no installation.

If Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
