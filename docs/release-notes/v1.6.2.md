## Taskbar Battery Widget 1.6.2

> 1.6.2 is the first published build of the 1.6 line (the 1.6.0 and 1.6.1 builds failed their CI tests and were never released).

### What's new — choose where the widget goes
*Settings ▸ Personalization ▸ Position*:
- **Taskbar, right** (next to the tray, as before) or **Taskbar, left** (needs centred Windows icons; with
  left-aligned icons the app tells you and keeps it on the right). Works with the taskbar on any edge.
- **Monitor**: show it on the primary monitor or on any other one, using that monitor's taskbar. A monitor
  without a taskbar shows the widget floating instead of hiding it.
- **Floating (draggable)**: a small always-on-top widget with its own background. Drag it anywhere — it snaps
  to the nearest corner or edge and remembers its place per monitor. **Floating position** picks one of the 8
  positions from the menu (no dragging needed), **Reset position** brings it back to the bottom-right corner.
- **Visible in fullscreen**: keep the widget over fullscreen apps (off by default).
- Plugging or unplugging a monitor moves the widget to the right place automatically.

### Changed
- Fullscreen detection is per monitor when you have more than one: a game on one monitor no longer hides the
  widget on the other.
- Small fixes: the panel's *+N* pill can be dragged in floating mode; a drag interrupted by Win+L or Alt+Tab no
  longer freezes the widget.

### Known limitations
- *Left* cannot know how many apps are on a centred taskbar, so it keeps to the left quarter of the taskbar.
- *Visible in fullscreen* works over borderless/windowed fullscreen, not over exclusive DirectX fullscreen.
- While Windows 11 Quick Settings, Start or notifications are open, the widget is hidden (system layer).

### Update
- From 1.4.0 or 1.5.x: *About… ▸ Check for updates ▸ Download and install* (signed update), or install the
  Setup below. Settings are kept; the default position is the same as before.

### Verify your download
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the SHA-256 of both executables; `SHA256SUMS.txt.sig` is its Ed25519 signature (see
  SECURITY.md for the public keys and manual verification).
- `gh attestation verify <file>.exe --repo Gabrielfch/taskbar-battery-widget` checks that the file was built by
  this repository's workflow.

### Downloads
- **TaskbarBatteryWidget-Setup-1.6.2.exe** — installer (upgrades previous versions and keeps your settings).
- **TaskbarBatteryWidget-portable-1.6.2.exe** — single executable, no installation.

The executables are not Authenticode-signed: if Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info → Run anyway**.
